About Me

The Story Behind The Lens

More than just a camera operator — I'm a storyteller, a visual artist, and someone who genuinely cares about capturing the moments that matter most.

5+ Years Experience

Hi, I'm Kay Nkhoma

I'm a Videographer, Content Creator and Camera Operator based in Lusaka, Zambia. For over five years I've been turning fleeting moments into timeless visual stories.

What started as a fascination with storytelling through moving images has grown into a full creative practice — capturing weddings, documenting lives, filming campaigns and creating content for brands, churches and individuals across Zambia.

Every project I take on, no matter the size, is treated with the same care, attention to detail, and creative energy. Because I believe every story deserves to be told beautifully.

My Journey

From first frame to full production — here's how it all came together.

2020

The First Frame

Picked up my first camera and discovered the magic of storytelling through moving images. Started filming small events for friends and family in Lusaka.

2021

Going Professional

Launched Kay Official TGD CHEZZI as an official creative brand. Shot my first weddings, corporate events and church productions across Zambia.

2022

Leveling Up

Invested in professional-grade gear and expanded services to include documentaries, drone coverage, and social media content for businesses and creators.

2023

Into The Air

Added aerial videography to my offering. From weddings in the bush to city skylines — drone footage opened up a whole new dimension of storytelling.

2024

Trusted By Many

Worked with churches, businesses, organizations and individuals on over 200 projects. Built a reputation for reliability, creativity and fast delivery.

2025 → Today

Storytelling Reimagined

Continuing to push creative boundaries — cinematic wedding films, brand documentaries, short-form content and full production support for major events.
