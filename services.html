Pricing Questions

Answers to the most common questions about booking and pricing.

Do you offer custom packages?
Yes. If the Starter, Standard, or Premium packages don't fit your needs, I'll create a custom quote based on your specific project. Just reach out with the details.
Do you travel outside Lusaka?
Absolutely. Projects outside my usual working area can be arranged. Travel costs depend on location and requirements — I'll include them in the custom quote.
What's your payment structure?
To secure your date, a 50% deposit is required. The remaining 50% is due on delivery of the final edited video. For larger projects, I can arrange a 3-payment plan.
How long is the turnaround time?
Standard turnaround is 2–3 weeks depending on project scope. Need it faster? Add "Rush Delivery" for a 48-hour turnaround. Wedding films typically take 3–4 weeks.
Do you provide the raw footage?
Raw footage can be provided on request for an additional fee. This includes all unedited clips, delivered on a USB drive or cloud link.
What equipment do you use?
I use professional cinema-grade equipment — including full-frame mirrorless cameras, prime lenses, pro lighting, and DJI drones for aerial work. Everything is maintained to broadcast standards.
