Before You Book

A few things worth knowing.

How quickly do you respond?
I respond to all enquiries within 24 hours. For urgent bookings, message me directly on WhatsApp for the fastest reply.
Do you require a deposit?
Yes — a 50% deposit secures your date. The remaining balance is due upon final delivery of your edited video.
How far in advance should I book?
For weddings and large events, 4–6 weeks minimum is recommended. Smaller shoots can often be arranged with a week's notice.
Do you travel outside Lusaka?
Absolutely. I regularly travel for weddings, events and shoots across Zambia. Travel and accommodation costs are added to the quote based on your location.
What do I need to prepare?
Once we confirm the booking, I'll send you a short prep guide with everything — from shot list ideas to timing recommendations. You don't need to worry about the technical side.
